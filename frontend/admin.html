Danger zone

v1 has no destructive actions and no fund-moving buttons here — nothing on this page moves real money. Live-money operations stay manual and owner-side:

  • Funding hot wallets — treasury and escrow hot wallets are topped up from the cold wallet by the owner, by hand. The bot never touches the cold wallet.
  • Flipping live flags — done above, behind a typed confirmation.
  • Sweeping excess hot balance — if a hot wallet exceeds its cap, the owner sweeps the excess to cold manually.
  • Token launch — the coin is launched owner-side; the mint address is then pasted into Branding above.
  • Failed payouts — any live payout that fails leaves a NULL signature in the ledger; handle it manually before retrying.
  • Legal review — betting and buyback flows need legal review before any live flip.
